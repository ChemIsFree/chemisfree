# ChemIsFree

**Open tools and resources for chemistry and drug discovery.**

ChemIsFree is an open initiative making computational chemistry and drug-discovery tools **freely accessible, discoverable, and reusable**.

We want to make it easier for researchers to find useful tools, for developers to build new ones, and for the community to share and improve the software and resources they use.

## What is ChemIsFree?

ChemIsFree brings together tools, projects, and resources relevant to chemistry and drug discovery under one open ecosystem.

This includes areas such as:

* Cheminformatics
* Computational chemistry
* Molecular modelling
* Virtual screening
* Drug discovery
* Structure-based and ligand-based methods
* Data and databases
* Machine learning and AI for chemistry
* Laboratory and research utilities

Some projects may be developed directly through ChemIsFree, while others may be independent projects that choose to be listed and shared through the ChemIsFree ecosystem.

## Build. Share. Discover.

There are several ways to be part of ChemIsFree.

### Build

Have an idea for a useful chemistry or drug-discovery tool?

You can contribute to an existing ChemIsFree project or collaborate with others to build something new.

### Share

Already have a tool or project?

ChemIsFree can provide a place for researchers to discover it and, where appropriate, include it within the wider ChemIsFree ecosystem.

Independent creators **retain ownership of their work** and receive visible credit and attribution.

### Curate

Help researchers find useful resources by contributing to curated lists of software, databases, workflows, tutorials, and other tools.

You don't need to be a software developer to contribute.

## Our approach

ChemIsFree is not about owning people's work.

It is about making useful tools **easier to build, find, access, share, and reuse**.

We believe that chemistry and drug discovery benefit when useful software and resources are easier to discover and when researchers can collaborate across disciplines.

## Who is ChemIsFree for?

ChemIsFree is for anyone interested in open tools for chemistry and drug discovery, including:

* Researchers
* Chemists
* Computational scientists
* Developers
* Students
* Tool creators
* Data scientists
* Curators
* Designers and technical communicators
